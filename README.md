Neon Bay is an original open-world crime game set in a neon-drenched harbor city at dusk. Explore on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, outrun the police, and run five story missions — from your first wheels at Ray's Garage to the final Harbor Heist. The city is alive with traffic, pedestrians, gang turf, a spray shop that clears your wanted level, and a wanted system that brings cop cars and foot patrols down on you the hotter you get.

Everything is original: the city, characters, vehicles, story, art and synthesized sound. No libraries, no network requests — just static files and Web Audio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to steal or enter a car, F to get out, Esc to pause, M to mute. Progress autosaves in your browser.
