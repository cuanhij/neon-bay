**Neon Bay** is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely offline in a desktop browser — plain canvas, no libraries, no network requests, all code, art, names and sound made for this game.

You are a newcomer in Neon Bay, a neon-lit harbour city at dusk. Drive five kinds of car (sedan, taxi, truck, sports car, police interceptor) with drift and handbrake, steal traffic cars off the street, outrun the Bay PD across a 0–5 star wanted system, and run five story missions for Rico and Mara: a first-wheels tutorial, a getaway chase, a 90-second timed delivery, a turf war against five Viper gang shooters, and a final harbour heist. The city has a seeded street grid, lit buildings and neon signs, a harbour pier, a garage, a hospital, a spray shop that clears wanted for $200, and a nightclub. Traffic drives the streets, pedestrians walk the sidewalks, and foot police chase and shoot back. Combat is mouse-aim pistol fire with limited ammo, health/ammo/cash pickups, muzzle flash, particles and screen shake. The HUD shows health, cash, ammo, wanted stars, objective, speedometer, clock and a live minimap. There is a title screen with New Game / Continue, pause, game over, mission-complete and story-complete screens, localStorage autosave, mute with M, and fully synthesized Web Audio (engine hum, gunshots, sirens, pickup blips and a bass music loop) — no audio files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

Controls: WASD move / drive, mouse aim, click shoot, E enter or steal a car, Shift run, Space handbrake, Esc pause, M mute.
